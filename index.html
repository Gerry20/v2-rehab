What we treat

Four specialties, one approach: get you moving properly again

Every plan starts with a proper assessment — not a generic exercise sheet. Vignesh builds the program around your body, your sport or job, and your actual goal.

Sports Injury & Performance

Return-to-play rehab, strength & conditioning, taping and pre-season screening for athletes and weekend players alike.

  • Ligament & muscle injury rehab
  • Return-to-sport progressions
  • Strength & mobility programming

Ortho & Post-Surgical Rehab

Structured recovery after joint, ligament or spine surgery, and hands-on management of fractures and joint pain.

  • Post-op knee, shoulder & spine
  • Fracture & joint-replacement rehab
  • Manual therapy

Neuro Rehabilitation

Gait retraining and functional recovery work for stroke, and other neurological conditions affecting movement.

  • Stroke & neuro recovery
  • Gait & balance retraining
  • Functional mobility training

General Musculoskeletal Pain

Back and neck pain, posture correction for desk workers, and everyday joint or muscle pain that's been ignored too long.

  • Back & neck pain
  • Posture & desk-job correction
  • Chronic joint pain management

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?
No — you can book directly. If your condition needs imaging or a specialist opinion first, Vignesh will tell you at the assessment and point you the right way.
How many sessions will I need?
It depends entirely on the condition — you'll get a realistic estimate and a plan with milestones after the first assessment, not an open-ended commitment.
What should I bring to the first session?
Any scans, reports or discharge summaries you have, a list of current medications, and clothes you can move comfortably in.
Do you treat children or elderly patients?
Yes — general musculoskeletal and post-surgical rehab is offered across age groups. Mention age and condition when you reach out so the session can be planned properly.
How far do home visits go?
Coverage areas are listed in the Clinic & Home Visits section above — message on WhatsApp to confirm your locality.
What are your fees?
See the Clinic & Home Visits section, or ask directly on WhatsApp — happy to quote before you book anything.
